Global Research Proposal - BIT/Nestlé
AI-Driven Multimodal Accessibility Platform for Inclusive Workplace 
Communication
Background
In large multinational organizations like Nestlé, communication relies heavily on 
visual and audio content—ranging from presentations and scanned documents to 
video meetings. However, employees with visual or hearing impairments face 
barriers in accessing this information. An integrated AI-driven accessibility platform, 
leveraging Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and Speech-to-Text (STT), can 
ensure inclusivity by automatically extracting and transcribing content, while also 
adapting outputs to individual needs.
This research initiative will be split into three complementary subtopics, each 
addressing a critical AI challenge, with the final goal of delivering a unified 
accessibility platform integrated into corporate tools such as Outlook, Teams, and 
websites.
Topic 1: Deep Learning for OCR in Real-World 
Corporate Documents
Objective:
To design, evaluate, and fine-tune deep learning OCR models for extracting text 
from diverse corporate visual materials (e.g., slides, PDFs, scanned reports, 
screenshots).
Research Questions:
1. How can OCR models be optimized to handle noisy, low-resolution, or 
multilingual corporate documents?
2. What fine-tuning strategies best adapt OCR models to Nestlé-specific 
vocabulary (brands, technical terms)?

===== PAGE 2 =====
3. How can OCR outputs be structured for seamless integration with 
accessibility tools (screen readers)?
Methodology:
● Conduct literature review on deep learning-based OCR.
● Build a dataset of corporate-like documents (scans, screenshots, slides).
● Benchmark models such as TrOCR, LayoutLM, Donut, and Tesseract.
● Apply transfer learning and fine-tuning with domain-specific data.
● Evaluate with Character Error Rate (CER), Word Error Rate (WER), and 
accessibility compliance.
Expected Outcome:
A fine-tuned OCR pipeline capable of extracting accurate text from complex 
workplace visuals, ready for integration into Outlook, Teams, and web platforms.
Topic 2: Advanced Speech-to-Text Models for Noisy, 
Multilingual Meetings
Objective:
To develop robust AI-based STT systems that can accurately transcribe corporate 
meetings and video calls in real time, handling multiple speakers, background noise, 
and multilingual content.
Research Questions:
1. How do state-of-the-art transformer-based ASR models perform in noisy and 
multilingual corporate settings?
2. Which adaptation strategies (transfer learning, domain-specific fine-tuning) 
improve transcription accuracy for workplace conversations?

===== PAGE 3 =====
3. How can real-time transcription latency be minimized without sacrificing 
accuracy?
Methodology:
● Literature review on advanced ASR methods.
● Collect/curate datasets simulating corporate meetings with multilingual and 
accented speech.
● Evaluate ASR models such as Whisper, wav2vec 2.0, and Conformer.
● Experiment with noise reduction, speaker diarization, and domain adaptation.
● Test prototypes for real-time transcription in simulated Teams meetings.
Expected Outcome:
A real-time STT engine optimized for corporate communications, capable of 
delivering accessible transcripts for hearing-impaired employees.
Topic 3: AI-Driven Personalization and Accessibility 
Layer
Objective:
To design an intelligent accessibility interface that personalizes OCR and STT 
outputs, making them adaptive, explainable, and inclusive for employees with 
diverse accessibility needs.
Research Questions:
1. How can AI personalization improve accessibility for visually and hearing￾impaired users?
2. What explainable AI methods (confidence indicators, error highlighting) 
increase trust in AI outputs?

===== PAGE 4 =====
3. How can reinforcement learning from user feedback continuously improve 
accessibility features?
Methodology:
● Literature review on personalization, explainable AI, and adaptive accessibility 
design.
● Prototype interfaces with customizable features (live captions, adjustable 
fonts/contrast, multi-language subtitles).
● Integrate confidence scoring and error feedback loops.
● Conduct user studies with accessibility tool users (screen reader users, 
hearing-impaired participants).
● Explore reinforcement learning approaches for adaptive improvement.
Expected Outcome:
An adaptive accessibility layer that transforms raw OCR and STT outputs into 
inclusive, user-friendly communication tools, ensuring compatibility with Outlook, 
Teams, and assistive technologies.
Final Integration
● Topic 1 (OCR Engine): Extracts text from images, scanned documents, and 
slides.
● Topic 2 (STT Engine): Transcribes spoken content from meetings and videos 
in real time.
● Topic 3 (Accessibility Layer): Adapts and personalizes the extracted content 
for inclusive use.
Together, these three research paths will produce a comprehensive AI-powered 
accessibility platform that fosters inclusivity, enhances communication, and 
supports Nestlé’s goal of creating an accessible workplace for all employees.
